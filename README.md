## 💡 Background, Solution, and Expected Effects

### Background & Challenges
While individual satisfaction exists within the current viewing experience, numerical disruption caused by bot activities and the inflation of evaluations are leading to the long-term dilution of content value.

### Solution
By reconsidering the premise that "voting = a free right" and introducing a physical cost (or restricted resources) to the evaluation process, we neutralize the illicit influence of bots and visualize only the evaluations driven by genuine interest.

### Expected Effects
- **Elimination of Noise:** Disrupting the economic rationality of bot activities through physical constraints to improve the purity of evaluations.
- **Rebuilding Trust:** Creating an environment where high-quality content receives proper exposure through a quality-assured evaluation system.
- **Platform Premiumization:** Transforming the platform from a state of chaotic "unrestricted freedom" to one characterized by the refined "privilege of selection."
